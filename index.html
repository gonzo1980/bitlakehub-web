The BLH-IA solution

From evidence to certificate, step by step

00 · The full picture

One core, from your evidence to the certificate

Documents and telemetry flow into a single evidence lake. BLH-IA analyzes them with two layers of AI and delivers what auditors and security teams need.

01 · Ingest

Everything in one evidence lake

Policies, inventories, risk registers, contracts and logs are collected, normalized and versioned, so every finding can be traced back to its source.

  • Documents
  • Inventories
  • Logs
  • Threat intel
02 · Orchestrate · Layer 1

The best external AI engines, coordinated

BLH-IA sends each task to the engine that does it best (reading documents, understanding language, classifying) and cross-checks the results before using them.

  • Document AI
  • Language models
  • Vision & OCR
  • Classifiers
03 · Learn · Layer 2

Proprietary models trained for security and regulation

Our own machine-learning models map evidence to ISO 27001 controls and ENS measures, correlate threats across sources and score residual risk.

  • Control mapping
  • Threat correlation
  • Residual risk
04 · Deliver

Audit-ready outputs and real-time response

Statement of Applicability, remediation plan, risk map and prioritized alerts. Every cycle feeds the next, so your security posture keeps improving.

  • SoA
  • Remediation plan
  • Risk map
  • Alerts
